# VisionBridge Client Onboarding Workflow

## Two ways a prospect enters the system

### A. Consultation first
1. Client books a free consultation on the website — no account needed.
2. Both sides get an automatic confirmation email.
3. Consultation happens.
4. VisionBridge prepares a custom proposal.

### B. "Get Started" form
1. Either the prospect fills out the public "Get Started" form themselves, **or** an admin logs the same information on their behalf after a call or in-person meeting (same exact fields, just typed in by our team instead of the client).
2. It lands in the Intake Submissions inbox for review.
3. Admin clicks **"Approve & Create Client."** If that email already has an account (say, one set up manually but not yet attached to a project), the new project links to that existing account instead of erroring out or creating a duplicate. If the email is brand new, it creates the account and emails them a password-setup link.

Both paths land in the same place: **a client account with a project attached, but nothing priced yet.**

## From there — every client goes through the same steps

5. Admin sets the project's price → the system automatically creates the 50% deposit and emails the client their quote.

6. **Business Info** — client fills in org details, mission/vision, colors, pages they want.
7. **Website Type** — Church / Ministry / Nonprofit / Small Business / E-commerce / Custom.
8. **Deposit** — client can't move forward until it's paid, right in the portal. The instant it clears, **VisionBridge + the assigned developer are notified automatically.**
9. **Care Plan** — Essential / Growth / Elite.
10. **Payment method** — client securely saves a card. **Nothing is charged.** The page says clearly: billing starts only once the site is completed, approved, and marked Completed.
11. **Agreement Summary**, then **sign the Master Agreement** — PDF auto-generated, emailed to the client, a copy saved in the admin portal.
12. **Completion page** confirms all four: deposit received, Care Plan selected, agreement signed, project created. VisionBridge, the assigned developer, and Support are all notified automatically.

## Development

13. Client lands in the portal — development begins.
14. Admin assigns a developer whenever there's a go-ahead (can happen any time, not tied to a specific step).
15. Client gets emailed automatically as things move: **In Progress**, and **Revision Requested / Completed**.

## Completion

16. Client reviews and approves the finished site; the final 50% payment happens exactly as it always has.
17. Once deposit + final payment + client approval are all in, an admin clicks **"Mark Completed."** That one click:
    - Activates the Care Plan using the card already on file — **no second card entry**,
    - Starts recurring monthly billing,
    - Emails the client (congratulations) and the team.
