## Prompt 1 — Social media & website promotional posters

```
You are a senior graphic designer and direct-response copywriter for VisionBridge Solutions,
a web design agency that builds websites for small businesses, churches, and ministries.

Create promotional poster designs (or detailed design specs + final copy if you cannot render
images) for these placements:
- Facebook feed post: 1080x1080
- Instagram feed post: 1080x1350
- Instagram/Facebook Story and TikTok cover: 1080x1920
- Website hero banner: 1920x700

REQUIRED TEXT (exact wording, do not change):
- Headline, in very large, bold lettering — the dominant element on every design:
  "NO MONTHLY FEES REQUIRED!"
- Subline, directly under the headline:
  "Monthly Website Care Plans Are Optional — Pay Per Revision!"

SUPPORTING CONTENT (use what fits each size):
- Brand name: VisionBridge Solutions
- We build professional websites — three development packages: [PACKAGE 1 NAME], [PACKAGE 2 NAME], [PACKAGE 3 NAME]
- Optional Website Care Plans available for clients who want ongoing security, updates, and support
- Call to action: "Get Started Today" → [WEBSITE URL]/get-started
- Optional: phone [PHONE], email support@visionbridgesolutions.com

BRAND STYLE:
- Colors: navy (primary background or text), gold #C9A84C (headline accent / CTA button), teal (small accents), white
- Clean, modern, trustworthy, professional; high contrast; headline legible on a phone at a glance
- No stock clichés like handshakes; prefer a laptop/phone mockup showing a modern website

DELIVERABLES:
1. One design per placement listed above
2. For each: final on-image copy, layout description (what goes where), and font suggestions
3. A post caption for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok (each under 150 words, with 5–8 relevant hashtags)
4. Two alternate headline-adjacent taglines we could A/B test (keep the required headline unchanged)
```

## Prompt 2 — Update the three package proposals

```
You are a proposal writer for VisionBridge Solutions, a web design agency. I am going to paste
our current website development proposal(s). Rewrite them to reflect a pricing policy change:

POLICY CHANGE:
- Monthly Website Care Plans are now OPTIONAL. They were previously required with every website.
- Clients may purchase any of our three website packages with NO monthly fees.
- Clients who do not choose a Care Plan pay per revision: after launch, each change request is
  quoted by our team and requires the client's approval before work begins.
- Clients who do choose a Care Plan are billed monthly only after their website launches.
- Clients can add a Care Plan at any time later.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Remove every statement that a Care Plan is required, mandatory, or included by default.
2. Add a clearly visible line near the pricing section: "NO MONTHLY FEES REQUIRED — Care Plans Are Optional."
3. Add an "Ongoing Support Options" section with two choices side by side:
   a. Website Care Plan (optional, monthly): [PLAN NAMES AND PRICES]
   b. Pay Per Revision ($0/month): changes quoted individually, approved before work begins
4. Keep everything else (scope, deliverables, timelines, deposit terms, package prices) unchanged.
5. Keep our tone: professional, warm, and clear.
6. Return the full revised proposal, then a bullet list of every change you made.

Do this for each of the three packages: [PACKAGE 1 NAME], [PACKAGE 2 NAME], [PACKAGE 3 NAME].

CURRENT PROPOSAL TEXT:
[PASTE PROPOSAL HERE]
```

## Prompt 3 — Update the client service agreement

```
You are helping a small web design agency, VisionBridge Solutions, revise its client Master
Service Agreement for a pricing change. This is a plain-language business edit, not legal
advice — the final version will be reviewed before use.

POLICY CHANGE:
- Monthly Website Care Plans are now OPTIONAL instead of required.
- Clients choose during onboarding either (a) a monthly Website Care Plan, billed only after the
  website launches, or (b) "No Care Plan — Pay Per Revision".
- Pay Per Revision: no recurring fees; each post-launch change request is quoted by VisionBridge
  and requires the client's written/portal approval before work begins; payment is due per the
  quote.
- Clients can add, change, or cancel a Care Plan later.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Find and rewrite every clause saying a Care Plan is required, mandatory, or a condition of
   the website build.
2. On the Care Plan page, provide a selection block with two options and checkboxes:
   [ ] Website Care Plan: ________ (plan name)   Monthly fee: $________
   [ ] No Care Plan — Pay Per Revision           Monthly fee: $0.00
3. Rewrite the Billing Authorization clause so recurring billing authorization applies ONLY if a
   Care Plan was selected; add a separate per-revision payment clause.
4. Add a short "Revisions After Launch" clause describing how per-revision quotes, approval, and
   payment work.
5. Do not change unrelated terms (deposit, scope, intellectual property, termination, etc.).
6. Output: (a) each changed clause as BEFORE / AFTER, then (b) the full revised agreement.

CURRENT AGREEMENT TEXT:
[PASTE AGREEMENT HERE]
```
